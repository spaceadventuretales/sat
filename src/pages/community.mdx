# Community And Contact

This page is the social and communication layer for Space Adventures.

## Discuss the universe

- Use the news posts to follow updates and share reactions.
- Open discussions and theory threads in the repository.
- Suggest lore clarifications and continuity fixes through issues.

## Social spaces

- GitHub repository: [spaceadvantures/sat](https://github.com/spaceadvantures/sat)
- News feed (RSS): [https://space-adventures.example.com/news/rss.xml](https://space-adventures.example.com/news/rss.xml)
- News feed (Atom): [https://space-adventures.example.com/news/atom.xml](https://space-adventures.example.com/news/atom.xml)

## Contact the creator

For direct project communication, use the repository issue tracker and mark your message with the `contact` label.

If private contact is added later, this page will include a dedicated channel.

## Collaboration guidelines

1. Keep feedback constructive and specific.
2. Separate canon facts from personal interpretation.
3. When proposing lore changes, include timeline impact.
