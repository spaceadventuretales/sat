# Why This Universe Exists

Space Adventures exists to build a long-term, coherent science-fiction universe where stories, films, and world reference material evolve together.

## Personal motivation

- Create a universe that rewards curiosity and long-term reading.
- Build stories that connect wonder, consequence, and human choices.
- Explore big themes through layered worldbuilding instead of isolated one-off plots.

## Project motivation

- Keep narrative and canon in one place so the universe can scale.
- Let readers follow stories while having a reliable encyclopedia for context.
- Publish progress transparently through the news feed and production updates.

## Long-term vision

- A living story platform that grows for years.
- Distinct arcs for tales and movies under shared continuity.
- A community that can discuss, challenge, and expand interpretation of the universe.
