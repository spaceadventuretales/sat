# Space Adventures Manifesto

This manifesto defines how stories are written and how videos are produced in this universe.

## Core principles

1. Canon continuity is mandatory.
2. Story quality is more important than output speed.
3. Every new entry must deepen worlds, characters, or themes.
4. Visual media must serve narrative meaning, not only spectacle.
5. Retcons are allowed only with explicit rationale and timeline updates.

## Writing approach

- Start with conflict and stakes, not exposition.
- Keep character decisions consistent with established motivations.
- Anchor every story to specific timeline windows.
- Connect each narrative to at least one world, faction, or technology thread.

## Video and movie approach

- Build from narrative beats to production choices.
- Keep visual language consistent with universe tone.
- Track canon-impacting scenes and synchronize encyclopedia updates after release.
- Publish production notes when a major story choice changes prior assumptions.

## Quality gate before publication

1. Continuity check against timeline and existing lore
2. Character behavior consistency check
3. Technical plausibility check for core universe rules
4. Editorial review for clarity, pacing, and thematic coherence
