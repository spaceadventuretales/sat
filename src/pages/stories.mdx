2. Canon fit check
3. Story draft
4. Continuity review
5. Canon publication

## Featured concept in development

### Lantern Coast Protocol

A migration convoy pushed off safe gate timing must cross the overstretched Lantern Coast rescue corridor while a navigator and the transit officer he distrusts uncover a manufactured transport crisis.

Canon anchors already in use:

- Pilgrim Mouth
- Lantern Coast
- Halcyon Aperture
- wrap-capable convoy operations

Story mode mix:

- Rescue Through Hazard
- Rival Pursuit
