# Named Ship Classes

This page defines reusable ship classes for the Space Adventures universe. These are not one-off hero ships. They are recurring hardware families that can appear across stories.

## Civilian And Commercial Classes

### Sparrow courier

- Role: urgent message transfer, diplomatic packet movement, elite passenger transport
- Profile: fast, lightly crewed, low endurance, high navigation quality
- Story use: time-critical missions, discreet travel, VIP movement

### Manta merchant

- Role: medium-volume trade across stable wrap corridors
- Profile: reliable, modular cargo spine, conservative field envelope
- Story use: merchant crews, customs stories, frontier contracts

### Pilgrim liner

- Role: civilian migration and long-range passenger service
- Profile: strong life-support redundancy, comfort-biased layout, predictable route discipline
- Story use: refugee arcs, pilgrimages, political evacuation, class tension

### Foundry hauler

- Role: industrial freight, heavy components, station construction loads
- Profile: massive cargo bias, slow local handling, dependent on escorts in risky regions
- Story use: logistics, colony building, labor politics, salvage

## Exploration And Frontier Classes

### Lantern surveyor

- Role: route charting, anomaly inspection, first-contact support, science missions
- Profile: advanced sensors, drone capacity, moderate self-defense, long independent endurance
- Story use: exploration, mystery, discovery, scientific dispute

### Hearth colony carrier

- Role: settlement transport and early-stage outpost support
- Profile: habitat-heavy, fabrication support, medical capacity, fragile strategic value
- Story use: colonization, fragile hope, convoy defense, supply crises

### Anchor rescue tender

- Role: emergency response, field recovery, tug and stabilization work
- Profile: heavy utility systems, medical and engineering teams, exceptional docking flexibility
- Story use: disaster response, rescue arcs, technical heroism

## Security And Military Classes

### Pike escort

- Role: convoy defense, patrol, anti-raider duty
- Profile: compact, durable, strong sensors for its size, long service life
- Story use: border friction, convoy action, policing contested lanes

### Ward patrol cruiser

- Role: regional security, anti-smuggling operations, political presence
- Profile: balanced combat and endurance, capable independent deployment
- Story use: tense inspections, local war, sector governance

### Bastion fleet carrier

- Role: command and force projection
- Profile: large coordination spaces, strike-craft support, strategic wrap capability, heavy logistics burden
- Story use: war campaigns, political intimidation, evacuation cover

### Obelisk siege platform

- Role: gate denial, fortress assault, static theater domination
- Profile: enormous mass, poor flexibility, terrifying local effect
- Story use: escalated war, blockade, existential threat

## Canon Guidance

These classes are broad enough to support multiple builders, regional variants, refits, and generational upgrades.

For example:

- a Sparrow courier from a wealthy core world may be elegant and overengineered
- a frontier Sparrow may be rugged, improvised, and barely legal
- both remain recognizably the same class family

## How To Use These Classes In Stories

- Use civilian classes to show economic and social texture.
- Use exploration classes to open mystery and expansion arcs.
- Use patrol and escort classes to ground law and violence.
- Use capital classes sparingly so they keep narrative weight.
