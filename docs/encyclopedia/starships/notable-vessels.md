# Notable Vessels

This page defines named vessels that can recur across stories and establish the tone of the major ship classes.

## Civilian And Commercial Ships

### Swift Petition

- Class: Sparrow courier
- Reputation: the benchmark diplomatic courier of the inner systems
- Known for: impossible delivery schedules, austere interiors, and legendary pilots
- Story use: sealed orders, urgent summons, political races

### Open Ledger

- Class: Manta merchant
- Reputation: honest trader in dishonest space
- Known for: surviving three embargo regimes and still turning a profit
- Story use: merchant ensemble cast, customs pressure, frontier deals

### House of Dawn

- Class: Pilgrim liner
- Reputation: beloved migration ship and occasional symbol of social inequality
- Known for: transporting generations of families to new colony waves
- Story use: family sagas, refugee crises, class conflict, civilian vulnerability

### Hammer Grace

- Class: Foundry hauler
- Reputation: ugly, indispensable, always overloaded by someone else's plan
- Known for: carrying station sections, reactor shells, and emergency reconstruction loads
- Story use: industrial stakes, convoy defense, labor politics

## Exploration And Frontier Ships

### Far Lantern

- Class: Lantern surveyor
- Reputation: first-choice ship for risky map extension and anomaly confirmation
- Known for: bringing back useful data and unsettling questions in equal measure
- Story use: discovery arcs, uncertain science, vanished crews, first-contact tension

### First Hearth

- Class: Hearth colony carrier
- Reputation: symbol of organized hope on the edge of civilization
- Known for: founding multiple viable settlements under hostile conditions
- Story use: colony origin stories, desperate logistics, political promises

### Mercy of Tides

- Class: Anchor rescue tender
- Reputation: the ship captains pray will answer their beacon
- Known for: field extractions from wreck clusters and unstable drop-out zones
- Story use: rescue drama, sacrifice, engineering heroism, aftermath stories

## Security And Military Ships

### Pike Vigilant

- Class: Pike escort
- Reputation: small enough to underestimate, stubborn enough to regret it
- Known for: protecting freight through the Ember Run during raider surges
- Story use: convoy action, patrol crews, asymmetric combat

### Ward Palecrest

- Class: Ward patrol cruiser
- Reputation: feared by smugglers, resented by local strongmen, admired by inspectors
- Known for: enforcing transit law across mixed-jurisdiction sectors
- Story use: law, corruption, inspections, limited-war tension

### Bastion Resolute

- Class: Bastion fleet carrier
- Reputation: moving capital of a mobile fleet doctrine
- Known for: coordinating major gate-defense operations without needing fixed support immediately nearby
- Story use: campaign command, political symbolism, fleet-scale drama

### Obelisk Null Crown

- Class: Obelisk siege platform
- Reputation: the kind of weapon whose arrival changes negotiation before it fires
- Known for: forcing corridor closures and terrifying fortified systems
- Story use: escalation, deterrence, siege, moral panic

## Canon Guidance

These ships should be treated as famous examples, not unique unrepeatable miracles. They anchor tone.

- A story may mention a Manta merchant and let readers picture the Open Ledger class of life.
- A war story may invoke the Bastion Resolute to imply fleet-level seriousness.
- A rescue arc can reference the Mercy of Tides even when that exact hull is elsewhere, because it defines the ideal.
