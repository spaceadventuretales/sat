# Armor, Hulls, And Structural Survival

Even the best field shields fail eventually. That is why passive survivability remains essential.

## Core Idea

Good armor is not simply thickness. Good survivability means that when something penetrates, the system does not die all at once.

## What Survivable Structures Need

- layered armor with different failure modes
- compartmentalization and isolation
- pressure-loss resilience
- thermal and radiation protection
- shock management and structural redundancy
- graceful degradation rather than catastrophic collapse

## Why This Matters

Ships, habitats, and cities in hostile environments cannot assume perfect conditions. Armor is often the difference between damage and extinction.
