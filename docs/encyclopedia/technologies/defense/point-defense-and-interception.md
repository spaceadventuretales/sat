# Point Defense And Interception

The most efficient way to survive a hit is often to ensure the hit never arrives.

Point-defense systems form the fast-reaction layer between detection and impact.

## Typical Threats

- missiles and torpedoes
- attack drones and smart munitions
- high-speed orbital debris
- hostile boarding or breaching craft
- improvised impact weapons

## Typical Tools

- rapid-fire interceptors
- beam or pulse weapons
- electronic spoofing and target confusion
- decoys and false signatures
- autonomous defensive swarms

## Why AI Matters Here

Interception is one of the most AI-dependent defensive domains because reaction time and target discrimination often exceed unaided human capability.
