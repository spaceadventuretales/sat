# Stealth And Signature Management

In an advanced civilization, hiding does not mean becoming literally invisible. It means controlling what can be inferred about you.

## Signature Sources

Most serious systems reveal themselves through some combination of:

- heat
- radiation
- electromagnetic emissions
- wake effects or field disturbances
- communications leakage
- predictable motion patterns

## What Signature Management Tries To Do

- suppress emissions
- redirect or diffuse detectable output
- mimic harmless or ordinary objects
- create false targets
- exploit sensor blind spots and relay gaps

## Why Stealth Still Matters

Even in sensor-rich civilizations, information is never free. Distance, clutter, infrastructure gaps, and deliberate deception preserve stealth as a live strategic problem.
