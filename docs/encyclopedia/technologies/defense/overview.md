# Defense And Shielding Systems

As civilization becomes more powerful, it also becomes easier to destroy. Advanced defense technology is therefore not only about war. It is about survival against accident, piracy, sabotage, bombardment, vacuum, radiation, hostile biospheres, and infrastructure failure.

## Core Themes

- field shields and layered defensive envelopes
- armor and survivable hull design
- interception of missiles, debris, drones, and strike craft
- signature management, stealth, and sensor denial

## Canon Default

Defense in this universe works best as a layered problem. No single shield or armor type makes everything invulnerable. Survival comes from stacking fields, structure, maneuver, detection, redundancy, and doctrine.
