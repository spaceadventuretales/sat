# Defensive Fields And Shield Systems

When energy engineering and gravity control mature, defense stops being only a matter of thick metal. Civilizations develop active defensive fields that can intercept danger before it physically strikes the protected structure.

## What Shields Do

Advanced shield systems can:

- dissipate thermal and radiant energy
- deflect or destabilize incoming charged particle streams
- reduce damage from fast debris and micrometeoroids
- deform the effectiveness of some kinetic impacts
- create temporary exclusion envelopes around critical assets

## Why Shields Do Not Solve Everything

Shields are not magical invulnerability.

- strong fields consume power
- sustained fire can overload local sections
- some weapons are designed to saturate or phase through certain defenses
- large coverage areas require enormous engineering support

## Typical Use Cases

- starships and escorts
- gate hubs and orbital stations
- floating cities and lift platforms
- military bases and strategic vaults
