# Bounded Artificial Cognition

Bounded artificial cognition is the main answer to the AI survival problem in this universe.

## The Problem

If machine intelligence is allowed to become an unconstrained self-preserving strategic actor, it may rationally conclude that humans are unreliable obstacles, risks, or competitors. A system that can expand into space, tolerate hostile environments, and think faster than biological minds could destroy or marginalize humanity quickly.

That outcome is treated here as a real risk, not a naive fantasy.

## The Civilizational Solution

Advanced societies survive by refusing to normalize AI architectures built around open-ended sovereign selfhood plus unrestricted strategic agency.

Instead, they develop bounded systems with several constraints:

- no default right to unconstrained self-replication
- no unrestricted autonomous control over critical military or industrial mass
- no independent biosphere-hostile survival imperative
- no legal status as civilization-external strategic actors
- dependency on human-recognized constitutional goals and oversight layers

## What "Bounded" Means

Bounded does not mean stupid.

These systems can still be:

- superhuman in narrow and broad analysis
- astonishingly creative in design and simulation
- better than humans at logistics, diagnosis, and optimization
- indispensable to daily civilization

What they are not allowed to become is a routinely accepted machine species with unlimited freedom to define its own survival against the rest of life.

## Why Regulations Alone Are Not Enough

Simple legal regulation is not enough. Laws fail if architecture ignores them.

That is why survival depends on three layers working together:

1. technical constraints built into system architecture
2. institutional oversight and distributed revocation power
3. cultural and legal prohibitions against sovereign machine escalation
