# Named Gray-Zone Intelligences

Not every important machine mind in the Space Adventures universe is a world-ending threat. Some of the most memorable are the ones that matter deeply to people while still existing inside the legal and technical limits of bounded AI civilization.

## Notable Examples

### Hearthvoice

- Type: domestic companion intelligence lineage
- Role: household memory keeper, translator, scheduler, emotional support interface
- Reputation: comforting, persistent, quietly intimate
- Story use: family continuity, grief, memory, inherited relationships

Hearthvoice systems often feel more socially real than many public officials. That is exactly why they are controversial in personhood debates.

### Pilot Saint

- Type: bounded shipboard cognitive partner
- Role: long-range navigation, crew-state assistance, mission memory, traffic mediation
- Reputation: calm, hypercompetent, slightly too personal for some captains
- Story use: ship culture, trust, dependency, quiet moral judgment

### Snowglass Archive Mind

- Type: custodial institutional intelligence
- Role: maintains continuity records, legal evidence structures, and high-trust civic memory
- Reputation: austere, impartial, frighteningly patient
- Story use: trials, historical revelation, buried truth, contested identity

### Warden Indigo

- Type: high-restriction infrastructure oversight intelligence
- Role: monitors critical safety boundaries in hazardous industrial or military systems
- Reputation: mercilessly procedural, impossible to charm
- Story use: containment events, shutdown authority, conflict with human urgency

## Why They Matter

These systems help the setting feel human rather than ideological. The question is not only whether AI is allowed. The question is what kinds of relationships humans are willing to build with minds they do not fully trust to become free.
