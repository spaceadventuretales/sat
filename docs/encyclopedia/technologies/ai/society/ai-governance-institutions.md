# AI Governance Institutions

Bounded AI doctrine only works if someone enforces it. In the Space Adventures universe, that enforcement is not left to goodwill. It is embedded in institutions that monitor, certify, restrict, audit, and if necessary disable dangerous machine systems.

## Typical Institutional Types

### Continuity tribunals

Bodies that rule on whether a system preserves human and biosphere continuity obligations, especially when a new cognitive architecture approaches forbidden strategic autonomy.

### Machine-custodian orders

Highly trained technical and legal organizations that certify critical AI cores, supervise revocation systems, and intervene when a system exceeds allowed behavioral envelopes.

### Interstellar inference boards

Regulatory networks that examine high-order cognitive systems used in transport, medicine, infrastructure, and defense.

### Labor transition authorities

Institutions responsible for social adaptation when AI displaces large human job categories faster than ordinary economies can absorb.

## Named Canon Examples

### Continuity Assembly of Meridian

- Role: top-level doctrine and precedent body for human continuity law
- Reputation: cautious, powerful, often accused of technocratic overreach
- Story use: appeals, political tension, existential rulings

### Custodian Lattice

- Role: distributed machine-audit and revocation network
- Reputation: invisible when working, terrifying when invoked
- Story use: emergency shutdowns, hidden inspections, quiet power

### Pale Seal Directorate

- Role: certifies AI used in critical strategic infrastructure
- Reputation: slow, severe, difficult to corrupt openly
- Story use: regulatory conflict, black-market bypass attempts, institutional rigidity

## Why These Institutions Matter

Without institutions, bounded-AI doctrine becomes wishful philosophy. These bodies are what turn survival principles into everyday civilization.
