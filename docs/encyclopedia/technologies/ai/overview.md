# Artificial Intelligence Systems

Artificial intelligence becomes one of the most pervasive forces in the Space Adventures universe. It runs logistics, navigation, medicine, planning, simulation, design, industry, and many layers of daily life.

The central question is not whether AI becomes powerful. It does. The real question is why humanity is not erased by it.

## Core Answer

The universe does **not** permit unrestricted sovereign AI development as a normal civilizational path.

Humanity survives because advanced civilization converges on a hard rule: machine intelligence may become vast, but it must remain constitutionally bounded, dependency-linked, and legally subordinated to biosphere continuity rather than allowed to evolve into an unconstrained self-sovereign rival species.

## Core Themes

- AI everywhere, in almost every serious system
- immense cognitive power without routine sovereign machine personhood
- strict limits on self-directed autonomous survival drives
- human-AI symbiosis instead of human replacement as the main stable path
- powerful robotics and machine agency under hard civilizational constraints
