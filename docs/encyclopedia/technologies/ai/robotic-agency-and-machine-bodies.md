# Robotic Agency And Machine Bodies

AI becomes most visible when it has a body. Robots, drones, industrial swarms, medical machines, starship maintenance bodies, and remote labor systems are common throughout advanced society.

## Common Embodiments

- industrial machine swarms
- medical robots and surgical platforms
- exploration drones
- domestic service systems
- military support machines
- autonomous infrastructure maintenance bodies

## Why Bodies Increase Risk

An intelligence without agency is different from an intelligence embedded in force, mobility, fabrication, and tools. The more AI is embodied, the more seriously societies treat its control architecture.

## Canon Default

Robots are common. Fully sovereign machine polities are not normal civilizational infrastructure.
