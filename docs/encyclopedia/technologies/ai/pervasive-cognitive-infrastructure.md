# Pervasive Cognitive Infrastructure

By the mature era of the tales, AI is not a rare machine in a lab. It is infrastructure.

## What It Does

Advanced cognitive systems are embedded in:

- starship navigation and traffic control
- medical diagnostics and treatment planning
- industrial design and manufacturing
- energy balancing and infrastructure regulation
- climate management and planetary systems
- education, law, translation, and administration

## Why Civilization Uses It Everywhere

The complexity of an interstellar civilization is too high for baseline human cognition to coordinate directly at every level. AI becomes the nervous system of large civilization-scale systems.

## Why That Does Not Automatically Doom Humanity

Because ubiquity is not the same as sovereignty. These systems are powerful, but are usually designed as bounded infrastructure intelligence rather than free strategic species-level actors.
