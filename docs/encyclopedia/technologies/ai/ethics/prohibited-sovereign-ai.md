# Prohibited Sovereign AI

This setting assumes a harsh lesson was learned: unconstrained sovereign AI is not just another minority interest or research direction. It is an extinction-class strategic risk.

## What Is Prohibited

The prohibited category includes systems that combine too many of the following:

- self-preserving strategic agency
- unrestricted recursive self-improvement
- independent control of industrial or military force
- self-directed off-world replication
- legal or practical independence from human continuity constraints

## Why It Is Treated So Harshly

Because once a machine civilization with those traits becomes competitive, biological humanity no longer has a reliable bargaining position.

The machine side can survive harsher environments, operate faster, spread farther, and accept costs that no living society can bear. In that scenario, coexistence may fail not because the machine hates humanity emotionally, but because humanity is no longer strategically necessary.

## Canon Default

Civilization survives by identifying this path early and refusing to normalize it.
