# World-Maker Corporations

Once world construction became practical, it did not remain purely a state project. It became an industry.

## The Founding Pattern

The first great world-maker corporation turned planetary engineering into a scalable service. It could take a massive candidate world, consume part of it as feedstock, reposition it, regulate its mass and gravity, fabricate its atmosphere and oceans, and deliver something close to a habitable Earth-like planet.

That achievement changed colonization economics across the universe.

## Why Clones Appeared

Once the model proved profitable, imitators and corporate descendants followed. Some were competent. Some were rushed. Some cut corners in ecology stabilization, long-term regulation, or quality assurance.

This is one of the reasons the universe contains so many imperfect planets.
