# World-Building Powers And Failures

This section covers the changing institutional and historical layer around new-world engineering rather than the stable core technologies themselves.

These topics are separated from the root technology pages because corporations, successor firms, and categories of failed planets are likely to evolve heavily during manual worldbuilding.

## Current Entries

1. [World-Maker Corporations](./world-maker-corporations.md)
2. [Failed And Abandoned Worlds](./failed-and-abandoned-worlds.md)

## Scope

- planet-building corporations
- clone or copycat firms with weaker standards
- abandoned terraforming programs
- themed or divergent worlds from different eras
- historical scars of world construction at scale
