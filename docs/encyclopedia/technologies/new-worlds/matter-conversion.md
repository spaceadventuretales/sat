# Matter Conversion

The cornerstone of new-world engineering is the ability to treat matter not as fixed substance, but as stored structure and stored energy.

## Summary

- converts matter into usable energy at industrial scales
- reassembles source mass into other needed materials
- provides the resource and energy base for full planetary engineering
- makes remote world creation possible without shipping everything from elsewhere

## Why It Changes Everything

Without matter conversion, terraforming remains mostly about modifying existing environments slowly. With matter conversion, civilization can do something more extreme: harvest part of a planet or moon as raw feedstock and transform it into the energy and materials needed to rebuild the remainder into a habitable world.

This is the key leap.

## Main Uses In World Construction

- converting unwanted crust or mantle mass into energy for orbital repositioning
- rebalancing planetary mass and density
- synthesizing required atmospheric components
- producing oceans, mineral mixes, topsoil precursors, and biological feedstock
- fabricating structural and control hardware on site

## Why It Is Not Just Magic Recycling

The process is powerful, but not free of engineering constraints.

- conversion efficiency still matters
- source composition limits what is easiest to synthesize
- planetary stability must be managed during mass extraction
- biosphere chemistry still has to be tuned carefully after fabrication

## Relationship To Energy Systems

Matter conversion sits at the boundary between energy technology and planetary engineering. It depends on extreme energy-control systems, including annihilation-grade processes, and then turns that energy back into mass arrangement useful for life and infrastructure.
