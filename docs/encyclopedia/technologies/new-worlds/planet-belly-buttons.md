# Planet Belly Buttons

Most engineered worlds retain at least one major polar scaffold complex after construction. In common language these are called `planet belly buttons`.

The name is informal, but it survives because it fits: the structure is the scar and anchor point left by artificial planetary birth.

## What They Are

A planet belly button is usually a polar or near-polar complex containing:

- the original terraforming control center
- remaining world-regulation hardware
- deep infrastructure access shafts
- monitoring systems for climate, orbit, mass distribution, and biosphere health
- abandoned or repurposed industrial scaffolding from the creation era

## Why Poles Are Common

Polar placement is practical for several reasons:

- lower conflict with major population belts
- easier thermal and orbital regulation geometry
- cleaner access to planetary rotation management models
- less disruption of equatorial climate and hydrology planning

## After Terraforming

On successful worlds, the belly button becomes one of three things:

- an active planetary systems core
- a restricted technical preserve
- a symbolic historical site

On failed or abandoned worlds, it often becomes the most mysterious ruin on the planet.

## Story Value

Planet belly buttons are ideal for:

- hidden control rooms
- relic technologies
- failed ecological archives
- sealed historical records
- strange cults or local myths about the makers of the world
