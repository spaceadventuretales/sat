# Energy Systems

Advanced civilization in the Space Adventures universe is limited less by intelligence than by usable power. The great transport, medical, industrial, and military systems of the setting only become possible after energy technology moves far beyond chemical fuels and ordinary grid electricity.

## Core Themes

- annihilation power for the highest energy densities
- antimatter production, capture, transport, and safe use
- gravity engineering and antigravity field control
- distributed micro-fusion for routine vehicles and settlements
- high-density storage for pulse loads and strategic buffering
- beam and field-based power transmission across distance

## Canon Default

Energy abundance is real in advanced regions, but not infinite. The setting works best when high energy density exists alongside engineering constraints, infrastructure costs, and safety doctrine.
