# Power Authorities And Energy Blocs

Where energy becomes dense enough to move cities, feed warp fleets, and reset the balance of entire regions, control of energy stops being a utility concern and becomes a civilizational power structure.

## Common Energy Actors

### Grid authorities

Technocratic organizations that regulate local and interregional distribution, keep major storage systems balanced, and decide who gets curtailed during crisis.

### Annihilation combines

Industrial powers controlling antimatter production, reserve storage, and cask logistics. Their influence often exceeds that of normal utilities.

### Lift-platform consortia

The organizations that maintain antigravity mirror fields for cities, ports, and freight architecture. If they fail, gravity resumes voting rights.

### Frontier cell traders

Less formal, often semi-legal networks that keep micro-fusion and field hardware moving through regions ignored by core infrastructure.

## Why This Layer Matters

Energy politics shapes:

- access to high-end transport
- military readiness
- urban survival
- industrial independence
- regional inequality

## Canon Guidance

Treat names and alliances here as flexible. The pure technologies may be stable, but the powers that own, regulate, sabotage, or privatize them should remain open to manual refinement.
