# Named Energy Infrastructure

Transport and medicine may shape civilization visibly, but energy infrastructure is what lets them stay online. This page provides reusable named facilities and systems that can recur across stories.

## Notable Installations

### Helios Crown Relay

- Type: orbital power relay and beam-distribution node
- Role: routes high-output power from solar and reactor farms into inner-system industry
- Reputation: elegant, critical, never truly secure
- Story use: sabotage, energy rationing, industrial leverage, orbital crisis

### Black Vault Meridian

- Type: antimatter reserve complex
- Role: strategic storage and metered release for core-state annihilation systems
- Reputation: paranoid, classified, too important to mention casually
- Story use: theft, blockade, state secrecy, strategic panic

### Khepri Foundry Spine

- Type: antimatter production and deceleration complex
- Role: one of the major source chains for engineering-grade antimatter stock
- Reputation: dangerous, militarized, economically decisive
- Story use: industrial espionage, labor unrest, wartime target

### Aerial Bastion Lattice

- Type: floating-city lift platform network
- Role: stabilizes major airborne districts and freight habitats through antigravity mirror arrays
- Reputation: miraculous to civilians, terrifying to maintenance engineers
- Story use: infrastructure failure, class separation, weather catastrophe, insurgency

### Pale Current Trunk

- Type: superconducting intercontinental or interregional power backbone
- Role: transmits enormous stable loads into dense urban and industrial clusters
- Reputation: boring until it fails, then civilization notices immediately
- Story use: cascading blackout, economic warfare, repair missions

## Canon Guidance

Use named infrastructure when you want power to feel like geography rather than an invisible convenience. The setting gets stronger when important systems have names, reputations, and failure consequences.
