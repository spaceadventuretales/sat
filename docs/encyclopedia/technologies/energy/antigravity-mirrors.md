# Antigravity Mirrors

The antigravity mirror is one of the most visually transformative inventions in the setting. It is the reason civilization can build flying freight ships, hovering urban platforms, low-energy air traffic, and even airborne cities.

## Core Idea

An antigravity mirror is a field-engineered surface or array that does not create unlimited free energy. Instead, it counteracts or redirects gravitational load so effectively that maintaining lift can require very little continuing power after field establishment and stabilization.

That is why such systems can make massive aerial structures feasible without constant brute-force thrust.

## Why "Mirror"

The term comes from the way the system is understood by engineers and pilots: it behaves as if gravitational influence is being reflected, redirected, or rephased rather than merely pushed against.

## What It Enables

- atmospheric cargo ships with ocean-ship scale
- personal floating vehicles
- heavy industrial lifters
- static or slow-drifting sky platforms
- airborne districts or cities where economics justify them
- gravity-driven or gravity-assisted engines for specialized machinery

## Why It Does Not Break The Setting

The mirror is not a perpetual-motion machine.

- it still requires power to initiate and control the field
- large loads still demand careful stabilization
- drift, oscillation, and field collapse remain hazards
- propulsion, steering, and weather handling remain separate engineering problems

So the setting gets spectacular lift without erasing cost, risk, or infrastructure.

## Scientific Basis

Unlike the wrap engine, which is grounded in a real (if highly speculative) theoretical framework related to spacetime metric engineering, the antigravity mirror has no direct current physical theory to justify it.

Real-world evidence now confirms that antimatter falls downward under gravity just as ordinary matter does, so the device is not a side effect of antimatter physics either.

The setting treats antigravity mirror technology as an engineering discovery that future physics will eventually explain — in the same way that practical steam engineers worked with heat and pressure long before thermodynamics was formalized as a mathematical science. The technology works. The deep theory is not fully written yet.

## Relationship To Energy

The reason these vehicles can approach near-zero operational energy for lift is that the field shoulders most of the gravitational burden. Energy is still spent on control, movement, life support, payload systems, and safety margins. "Almost free flight" really means "lift is no longer the dominant expense."
