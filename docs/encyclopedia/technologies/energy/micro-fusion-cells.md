# Micro-Fusion Cells

Micro-fusion cells are the everyday backbone of advanced power. If annihilation power sits at the strategic extreme, micro-fusion cells sit at the practical middle: small enough to deploy broadly, strong enough to replace most older fuel logistics.

## Summary

- role: compact long-duration power for vehicles, homes, ships, tools, and settlements
- effect: most people stop thinking in terms of gas stations and ordinary battery charging
- limitation: still not a replacement for annihilation-grade peak field systems

## What They Replace

Micro-fusion cells eliminate or shrink dependence on:

- gasoline-like fuels
- ordinary rechargeable batteries for major systems
- routine long-range refueling stops for many vehicles
- fragile remote-grid dependence in developed regions

## Why They Still Matter If Bigger Reactors Exist

Because civilization needs distributed power, not only central generation.

Micro-fusion makes sense when you need:

- long endurance
- low maintenance intervals
- mobile independence
- much higher energy density than chemical storage

## Typical Uses

- private and commercial vehicles
- frontier settlements
- portable industrial equipment
- small spacecraft auxiliaries
- medical and rescue platforms

## Why Energy Storage Still Exists

Even with micro-fusion cells, storage still matters. Fusion excels at sustained power. Storage excels at instant burst delivery, redundancy, silent operation, and buffering.

That is why a mature civilization still uses both.
