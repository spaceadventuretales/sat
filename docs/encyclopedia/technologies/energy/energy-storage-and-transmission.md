# Energy Storage And Transmission

Even in a civilization with micro-fusion cells and annihilation plants, energy storage and transmission still matter enormously.

## Why Storage Still Makes Sense

Yes, it still makes sense.

Micro-fusion cells provide durable local generation, but they do not solve every power problem. Storage remains essential for:

- sudden peak loads
- weapons or shield pulses
- medical and life-support backup
- transport systems that need silent reserve power
- grid balancing and emergency recovery
- safe buffering between reactors and delicate systems

In short, generation and storage do different jobs.

## Typical Storage Forms

- high-density capacitor banks for instant discharge
- field batteries for transport and defense systems
- cryogenic or exotic-state storage modules for strategic reserves
- antimatter reserve packets for extreme applications

## Transmission

Long-range energy transmission becomes necessary whenever local generation is inefficient, politically restricted, or too dangerous to place near civilians.

Common transmission forms can include:

- beamed microwave or laser power
- guided plasma or field conduits
- superconducting trunk lines in core infrastructure
- orbital relay and collector platforms

## Why It Matters In Story

Power distribution creates geography just as transport does.

- some worlds export refined power
- some worlds import it through relay networks
- some ships carry generation but still depend on recharge infrastructure for high-intensity systems
- some military or frontier systems become valuable because they are energy nodes, not population centers
