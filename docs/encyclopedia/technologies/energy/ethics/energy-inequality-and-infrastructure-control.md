# Energy Inequality And Infrastructure Control

Abundant energy does not automatically produce equality. In the Space Adventures universe, advanced power can deepen hierarchy just as easily as it reduces scarcity.

## Typical Inequalities

- core regions get resilient grids while frontier systems patch themselves together
- elites gain access to secure annihilation-backed transport while ordinary populations depend on slower infrastructure
- floating cities may literally rise above the populations whose labor maintains them
- military and industrial actors can monopolize high-quality field systems

## Why It Matters

If transport shapes geography, energy shapes who gets to use geography comfortably.

## Story Use

This topic is useful for:

- class conflict
- infrastructure sabotage
- frontier resentment
- corporate or state monopoly tension
- disaster response shaped by unequal resilience
