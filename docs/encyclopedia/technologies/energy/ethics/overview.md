# Energy Ethics, Law, And Strategic Risk

This section covers the unstable interpretive layer around energy technology: who is allowed to possess it, who regulates it, how it can be abused, and what kinds of power concentration societies are willing to tolerate.

These pages are separated from the root energy technology layer because the engineering may remain stable while politics and ethics keep changing.
