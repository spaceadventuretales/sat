# Relay Lattices And Tightbeam Networks

Most serious interstellar communication does not spread equally in all directions. It is routed through infrastructure.

## Core Idea

Civilization relies on directional high-bandwidth beams supported by relay stations, orbital nodes, and corridor-linked communication lattices.

This makes communication:

- fast where infrastructure is strong
- weak where routes are poor
- vulnerable where hubs are attacked or isolated

## Why This Matters

If communication were effortless everywhere, distance would collapse too completely. Relay lattices preserve believable geography.
