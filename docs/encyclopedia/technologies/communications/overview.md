# Communication Systems

No interstellar civilization survives on transport alone. It also needs ways to move meaning, orders, warnings, law, and culture across enormous distances.

Communication technology in the Space Adventures universe is therefore not a minor utility. It is one of the pillars of civilization.

## Core Themes

- beam and relay communication across normal space
- physical courier transport for high-value or massive data
- translation and presence systems for multicultural civilization
- communication limits that preserve scale and stakes
