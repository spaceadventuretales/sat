# Travel Systems

Travel technology determines the real size of civilization.

In the Space Adventures universe, long-range mobility relies on two major ideas:

- [Wrap Engine](./wrap-engine.md): flexible faster-than-light travel for ships operating across ordinary space
- [Hole Teleportation](./hole-teleportation.md): fixed gate technology for rare, strategically prepared locations

## Why This Category Matters

Without advanced travel systems, even nearby stars remain politically and economically isolated. With them, trade, diplomacy, warfare, migration, and storytelling can happen on human timescales.
