# Major Gate Hubs And Frontier Corridors

This page names the most important transport nodes and routes in the Space Adventures universe. These entries are designed to be reused across stories as stable geography.

## Great Gate Hubs

### Crown of Meridian

- Type: Class III strategic junction gate complex
- Role: primary administrative and diplomatic interchange for the old inner worlds
- Reputation: efficient, overregulated, politically loaded
- Story use: summits, customs conflict, covert transit, elite intrigue

The Crown of Meridian is the model of a mature gate civilization: safe, rich, and never neutral.

### Nine Rings Exchange

- Type: multi-ring commercial gate hub
- Role: largest open trade convergence in the mid-core
- Reputation: lawful on paper, ruthless in practice
- Story use: merchant drama, cartel pressure, convoy assembly, industrial espionage

The Treaty of Nine Rings made the hub famous, but commerce made it indispensable.

### Halcyon Aperture

- Type: civilian-scientific high-stability gate nexus
- Role: research, medical transfer, precision diplomatic routing
- Reputation: calm, prestigious, hard to access without authorization
- Story use: secret research, protected transit, fragile political deals

### Bastion Khepri

- Type: Class IV fortress gate
- Role: military control of a disputed corridor fan
- Reputation: intimidating, disciplined, never relaxed
- Story use: blockade tension, fleet staging, wartime evacuation, sabotage

### Pilgrim Mouth

- Type: mass-passenger and migration gate hub
- Role: long-distance migration, religious travel, family reunification routes
- Reputation: humane but overwhelmed during crises
- Story use: refugee movement, social friction, mass departures, human-scale stakes

## Famous Frontier Corridors

### Ember Run

- Type: high-traffic wrap corridor
- Role: links core industry to semi-stable frontier markets
- Reputation: profitable, dangerous, crowded with escorts and smugglers
- Story use: merchant action, convoy defense, black-market logistics

### Glass Ladder

- Type: survey-born corridor through delicate navigation terrain
- Role: scientific access and limited high-value trade
- Reputation: beautiful, unforgiving, highly dependent on expert astrometrics
- Story use: exploration, route loss, technical drama, anomaly encounters

### Mourning Chain

- Type: old colonial corridor partly degraded by war and infrastructure loss
- Role: still usable, but socially and politically fragmented
- Reputation: haunted by history, rich in salvage, poor in trust
- Story use: ruins, war memory, independent settlements, buried weapons

### Horizon Thread

- Type: deep-range frontier corridor
- Role: thinnest viable line between mapped civilization and exploratory dark space
- Reputation: heroic to romantics, reckless to professionals
- Story use: discovery, rescue, first-contact pressure, long isolation

### Lantern Coast

- Type: humanitarian and rescue corridor
- Role: medical aid, disaster response, small-colony support, refugee extraction
- Reputation: respected, overstretched, chronically under-defended
- Story use: mercy missions, triage, convoy sacrifice, relief politics

## Dead-Zone Boundaries

### The Black Choir Margin

- Type: dead-zone boundary region
- Role: practical edge of dependable charted transit in one contested quadrant
- Reputation: sensor noise, missing ships, contradictory old maps
- Story use: lost expeditions, horror, covert facilities, unreliable witnesses

### Silent Vault Reach

- Type: quarantined denial belt
- Role: sealed-off region associated with old interdiction history
- Reputation: officially inaccessible, unofficially tempting
- Story use: conspiracies, relic hunts, unauthorized penetration

## Canon Usage Rules

- Core-world stories should refer to major hubs by name, not as generic gates.
- Frontier stories should name corridors to make remoteness feel structured rather than vague.
- Dead-zone stories should use boundary names to mark the edge between known transit and myth.
