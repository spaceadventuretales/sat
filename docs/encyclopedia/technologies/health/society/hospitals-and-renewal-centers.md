# Hospitals And Renewal Centers

Advanced medical technology needs institutions capable of applying it at scale. These facilities are not just hospitals in the old sense. They are reconstruction complexes, trauma salvage networks, genomic foundries, and long-term continuity custodians.

## Notable Examples

### Meridian Renewal Citadel

- Role: flagship body-renewal and continuity transfer center in the core worlds
- Reputation: prestigious, expensive, politically connected
- Known for: full-spectrum body renewal, high-end identity continuity protocols, elite clientele

### Lantern Coast Rescue Array

- Role: distributed emergency trauma and nanomedical stabilization network
- Reputation: humane, overstretched, heroic
- Known for: saving patients who should not survive long enough to reach major clinics

### Paleglass Organ Conservatory

- Role: organ growth, custom tissue fabrication, rare-body compatibility treatment
- Reputation: technically brilliant, socially cold
- Known for: solving cases too unusual for mass-market medicine

### Khepri Veteran Reconstruction Vaults

- Role: military reconstruction and cyborg rehabilitation system
- Reputation: effective, austere, psychologically difficult
- Known for: restoring severely damaged soldiers and integrating complex prosthetic-military bodies

## Story Use

- Use renewal citadels for elite medicine, political access, and social inequality.
- Use rescue arrays for humanitarian stakes and time-critical survival.
- Use specialty conservatories for rare biology, uplift care, and legal edge cases.
- Use reconstruction vaults for war aftermath, trauma, and difficult returns to civilian life.
