# Biotech Powers And Medical Factions

Where medicine can prolong life, rebuild bodies, and create new kinds of persons, control of medicine becomes control of society.

## Typical Power Blocs

### Renewal combines

Large corporate systems controlling body-printing platforms, genomic certification, premium transfer care, and cross-system medical licensing.

### Surgical orders

Professional guilds or quasi-monastic institutions that guard continuity protocols, brain-restoration doctrine, and difficult ethical standards.

### Uplift rights movements

Political organizations fighting for full recognition of uplifted and engineered peoples as persons with legal dignity rather than patented outcomes.

### Augmentation hardliners

Factions that promote aggressive body enhancement and reject the idea that biological baseline humanity should remain the norm.

### Purity or continuity conservatives

Movements that accept healing but resist radical redesign, repeated renewal, or identity experimentation.

## Why This Matters

These factions shape:

- who gets access to renewal
- who can legally redesign bodies
- whether uplifted species gain equal citizenship
- whether cloned mind-states count as persons
- how much augmentation a military can demand

## Canon Guidance

Treat names and alliances here as flexible worldbuilding material. Unlike the underlying technologies, institutions and factions can split, merge, fall, or be rewritten as the larger setting sharpens.
