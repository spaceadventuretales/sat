# Rapid Diagnostics And On-Demand Therapeutics

One of the biggest medical changes in the Space Adventures universe is speed of understanding. Many conditions that once required labs, time, and guesswork can now be recognized in minutes or seconds.

## Core Capabilities

- rapid detection of disease signatures
- identification of toxins, pathogens, and metabolic damage
- generation of custom antidotes or treatment compounds
- adaptation of treatment to the patient's exact biology

## Diagnostic Analyzers

Portable analyzers can scan for:

- viruses
- bacteria
- fungal agents
- toxins and industrial contamination
- radiation-linked tissue damage
- molecular markers of genetic instability

## On-Demand Medication Synthesis

Once the system knows what is wrong, treatment can often be synthesized locally. Instead of relying only on prepackaged drugs, advanced medical networks can produce targeted compounds on demand from validated formulas and live patient data.

This is especially important in frontier medicine, war zones, isolated stations, and contact with unfamiliar biospheres.
