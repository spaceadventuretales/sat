# Anabiosis

Anabiosis is the controlled suppression of metabolism and neural activity to preserve life during periods when ordinary biological operation would lead to death, damage, or waste of limited medical resources.

## Common Uses

- preservation after catastrophic trauma
- long-distance medical transport
- delayed surgery in extreme environments
- survival during evacuation failure or system disaster
- transport of high-value patients through dangerous routes

## Why It Matters

Anabiosis turns time into a medical resource. A patient who cannot be treated now may still be treatable later if their biology can be slowed safely enough.

## Relationship To Other Systems

Anabiosis often works together with:

- the [Last Chance Cube](./last-chance-cube.md)
- [Nanomedical Repair Systems](./nanomedical-repair-systems.md)
- [Body Renewal And Soma Transfer](./body-renewal-and-soma-transfer.md)

## Limits

- not all damage can be safely suspended
- longer suspensions increase complexity and risk
- different species tolerate the process differently
