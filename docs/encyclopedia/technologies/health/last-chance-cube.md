# Last Chance Cube

The last chance cube is a brutal but lifesaving emergency device used when the body cannot be saved and evacuation time is collapsing.

In plain terms, it is designed to sever, stabilize, seal, sedate, and preserve the head and brain long enough for later reconstruction or full body renewal.

## Summary

- use case: catastrophic injury with no viable full-body extraction
- goal: preserve the brain, identity continuity, and surgical viability
- common users: military medics, extreme rescue teams, deep-space crews, hazardous-industry responders
- reputation: horrifying, effective, controversial
- name origin: the outer housing is a rigid sealed cube designed to survive harsh environments; the working deployment assembly inside is collar-shaped and wraps around the upper cervical zone

## Operating Principle

When triggered by trained personnel or approved automatic criteria, the cube deploys around the upper cervical zone and performs a rapid sequence:

1. traumatic isolation of the head from the body
2. vascular sealing and neural protection within the first seconds, before circulatory failure progresses to brain death
3. metabolic suppression and deep sedation
4. environmental stabilization inside a protected medical shell

The device is calibrated to act faster than biological death timelines. Its integrated rapid-seal systems operate within the critical window before irreversible neural damage begins. This is why it is classified as a medical emergency device rather than a battlefield amputation tool: the engineering margin is real, but narrow.

Advanced versions can maintain the preserved patient in a sleep state or deep anabiotic suspension until definitive care is available.

## Why It Exists

The device emerged from a simple reality: in some battles, crashes, vacuum failures, toxic breaches, or industrial catastrophes, the body cannot be recovered in time, but the brain still can.

Combined with [Body Renewal And Soma Transfer](./body-renewal-and-soma-transfer.md), the cube turns certain otherwise-fatal injuries into delayed reconstruction cases.

## Social Meaning

The last chance cube is feared because it removes all comforting illusions about what advanced trauma medicine really costs. Survivors may bless it. Witnesses often hate it.

## Limits

- it is not pleasant, elegant, or routine civilian medicine
- severe brain damage can still defeat it
- misuse raises legal and ethical concerns
- some cultures ban nonconsensual deployment except under military law
