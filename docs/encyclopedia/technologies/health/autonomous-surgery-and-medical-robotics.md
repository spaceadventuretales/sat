# Autonomous Surgery And Medical Robotics

In advanced medical systems, most high-precision surgery is performed by robotic platforms rather than unaided human hands.

## Summary

- extreme precision through micro-incisions or internal access channels
- reduced trauma compared with older open-surgery methods
- better coordination with diagnostics, imaging, and nanomedical repair
- rapid recovery and lower complication rates

## Why Robots Dominate Surgery

Robotic systems do not get tired, do not shake, and can work at scales where old human-only surgery becomes needlessly destructive. They are especially effective when linked to live scans, programmable tool libraries, and internal repair nanotechnology.

## Typical Uses

- vascular reconstruction
- tumor extraction
- neural access support
- organ replacement and connection
- trauma salvage during transport
