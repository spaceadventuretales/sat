# Health, Longevity, And Biotech

Health technology in the Space Adventures universe goes far beyond treating wounds and disease. It changes lifespan, identity, recovery, body design, species creation, and the boundary between biology and machinery.

## Core Themes

- radical longevity without routine aging
- body replacement and organ regeneration
- trauma preservation and delayed rescue
- genetic redesign and uplift
- augmentation, cyborgization, and adaptive medicine
- brain preservation, restoration, and identity debates
