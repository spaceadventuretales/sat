# Bioethics And Continuity Law

Once medicine can rebuild bodies and alter species, the hardest questions are no longer technical. They are legal and philosophical.

## Core Disputes

- Is a renewed person legally continuous with the old body?
- How much DNA correction counts as therapy and how much counts as redesign?
- Do uplifted or designed peoples inherit full personhood automatically?
- How much augmentation can employers, militaries, or states demand?
- Is a recorded mind-state a person, property, evidence, or none of the above?

## Continuity Law

Most advanced systems distinguish between:

- preserved-person continuity through living brain transfer
- gradual restorative intervention with continuity safeguards
- clone or snapshot-derived persons, whose legal status is often disputed

This is one of the most important legal distinctions in the setting.

## Why It Matters In Story

Bioethics law can drive inheritance disputes, military coercion, citizenship conflict, religious tension, and criminal exploitation just as strongly as war or politics.
