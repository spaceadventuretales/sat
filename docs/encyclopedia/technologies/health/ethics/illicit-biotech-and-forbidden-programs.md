# Illicit Biotech And Forbidden Programs

Where powerful medicine exists, forbidden versions follow.

## Common Illicit Sectors

- black-market body renewal for fugitives and the ultra-rich
- illegal organ growth and counterfeit tissue certification
- coerced augmentation programs
- unregulated uplift or speciation labs
- mind-state capture and continuity fraud
- military experiments outside lawful medical doctrine

## Why These Programs Exist

Some actors want immortality without scrutiny. Others want soldiers, workers, or designer populations optimized without rights, oversight, or consent.

## Tone Guidance

Illegal biotech should feel dangerous not only because it is criminal, but because it often breaks the social protections that make advanced medicine humane.
