# Engineered Non-Humanoid Beings

Once biotechnology advances far enough, intelligence no longer has to live inside a familiar human-like body. Some of the strangest results of late bioengineering are beings designed around radically different physical organization.

## Common Families

### Field beings

Entities whose stable function depends on persistent bioelectromagnetic or exotic field organization coupled to a material substrate.

### Fluid beings

Entities built around distributed soft or semi-liquid biological architecture, often with remarkable shape adaptability and damage tolerance.

### Dynamic particle beings

Entities whose bodies rely on constantly reorganized microstructures rather than fixed anatomy, producing bodies that are stable only while active control systems remain coherent.

## Why They Matter

These beings force civilization to rethink medicine, law, personhood, architecture, and even the meaning of injury. A hospital built for humans may be useless to a field-organized patient. A prison cell, pressure suit, or chair may assume the wrong kind of body entirely.

## Origins

Some of these beings are intentional design projects. Others emerged from uplift or experimental adaptation programs meant to solve environmental survival problems that standard bodies could not handle well.
