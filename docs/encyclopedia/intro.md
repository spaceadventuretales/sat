# Space Adventures Encyclopedia

Welcome to the canonical reference layer for the Space Adventures universe.

Stories and movies live in their own sections. This encyclopedia exists to keep continuity stable while narrative content grows.

This reference is organized around:

- History and timeline continuity
- Galaxies, systems, planets, moons, and colonies
- Characters, species, plants, and civilizations
- Technologies, devices, and starships
- Factions, companies, ideologies, and regimes
- Events, festivals, and cultural milestones

## Primary narrative sections

- Stories hub: /stories
- Movies section: /movies

## How to use this encyclopedia

- Add major canonical entries in the relevant section.
- Link related entries across sections to maintain continuity.
- Mark content as draft, canon, or deprecated when lore changes.
- Keep names and dates consistent with the timeline in History.

## Suggested entry format

Use this minimal structure for each new page:

1. Summary
2. Canon status
3. Timeline placement
4. Relationships and cross-links
5. Sources or production notes
