# Canon Policy

This document governs how new tales interact with the established SAT universe.

## Canon hierarchy

1. Published encyclopedia pages
2. Published story and movie pages
3. Approved manifesto policies
4. Draft planning notes

If two sources conflict, the higher source wins until an explicit correction is made.

## Timeline rule

Every tale must declare its placement against the master timeline in `docs/encyclopedia/history/timeline.md`.

If the exact date is unknown, define at least:

- relative era
- key preceding event
- key following consequence

## Continuity rule

Every tale should connect to at least one established element:

- character
- world or territory
- faction or company
- technology or device
- historical event

## Revision rule

Retcons are allowed only when all three conditions are true:

1. The gain in story coherence or universe quality is meaningful.
2. The change is documented explicitly.
3. Related encyclopedia pages are updated together.

## Draft versus canon

- Draft: brainstorming, alternatives, abandoned concepts, private notes
- Canon: published or explicitly approved universe facts

Do not present drafts as settled truth.

## Tale handoff rule

When a tale introduces something canon-relevant, record follow-up work for the encyclopedia if needed.

Examples:

- new ship class
- new colony or station
- new political agreement
- new transport doctrine
- new social or legal practice

## Realism consistency

New stories may expand science, logistics, or institutions, but cannot casually ignore prior operating rules.

If a story needs an exceptional event, explain why it is exceptional and why it does not become routine everywhere else.
